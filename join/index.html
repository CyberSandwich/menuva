Add Your Restaurant to Menuva

将您的餐厅加入 Menuva

Effective date: 18 March 2026 · Last updated: 19 April 2026
生效日期：2026年3月18日 · 最后更新：2026年4月19日

Menuva is currently accepting selected requests to join the platform. Self-nomination will be available soon.

1. How to apply

  1. Visit admin.menuva.co.uk and sign in using Google or any email address. Email sign-in is passwordless.
  2. Email hello@menuva.co.uk with the email address used to sign in and proof that you are the owner or authorised representative of a legitimate food venue (for example, a business registration certificate, a Companies House listing, or a link to your venue's official website).
  3. Once your access is approved, the admin portal will guide you through adding your venue, including its name, opening hours, location, and menu items.

2. Menu creation support

Our team can support the creation of an initial draft of your menu. If you would like us to process your menu on your behalf, email hello@menuva.co.uk with the following.

Required (minimum):

  • Item name and price for each menu item.

Optional (recommended where available):

  • Hero images and item images.
  • Item descriptions.
  • Allergen and dietary information.
  • Customizations, including option names, prices, minimum and maximum quantities, and whether each selection is mandatory or optional.
  • Food hygiene rating.
  • Sections and subsections for menu organisation.

The more detail you provide, the more complete your menu will be from day one.

3. What to expect

  1. Menuva is in its early stages. The admin portal and onboarding experience are actively being refined.
  2. If you encounter any issues, find that the experience can be improved, or discover a bug at any point, please let hello@menuva.co.uk know as soon as possible. Your feedback directly shapes the platform.

Menuva 目前正在接受指定的入驻申请。自助申请功能即将开放。

1. 如何申请

  1. 访问 admin.menuva.co.uk，使用 Google 或任何电子邮件地址登录。邮箱登录无需密码。
  2. 发送邮件至 hello@menuva.co.uk，提供登录时使用的电子邮件地址，以及证明您是合法餐饮场所的所有者或授权代表的材料（例如营业执照、公司注册信息或餐厅官方网站链接）。
  3. 访问权限获批后，管理门户将引导您添加餐厅信息，包括名称、营业时间、位置和菜单项目。

2. 菜单创建支持

我们的团队可以协助创建菜单初稿。如果您希望由我们代为处理菜单，请发送邮件至 hello@menuva.co.uk，包含以下信息。

必需（最低要求）：

  • 每个菜单项目的名称和价格。

可选（建议尽可能提供）：

  • 主图和菜品图片。
  • 菜品描述。
  • 过敏原和饮食信息。
  • 定制选项，包括选项名称、价格、最小和最大数量，以及每个选项是必选还是可选。
  • 食品卫生评级。
  • 菜单分区和子分区。

您提供的信息越详细，菜单从上线第一天起就越完整。

3. 注意事项

  1. Menuva 仍处于早期阶段。管理门户和入驻流程正在持续优化中。
  2. 如果您遇到任何问题、发现体验可以改进或发现错误，请尽快联系 hello@menuva.co.uk。您的反馈将直接影响平台的发展。
