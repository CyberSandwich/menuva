2. Why join Menuva

Menuva is a student-led project at the University of Warwick with no revenue and no commercial activity. It is free for restaurants today and will stay free.

More diners discovering you

~2,000 active Warwick diners already use Menuva, with new users every week. 83% of diners research menus before visiting a restaurant, so being listed means your menu lands in front of them at the exact moment they are deciding where to eat.

Higher bookings and basket sizes

Venues with visible menus online see approximately 10% higher bookings. Menus with photos, descriptions, and allergen data consistently raise average order value, because diners arrive knowing what they want and feel confident ordering extras like sides, drinks, and desserts.

International and restricted-diet diners

Menus are auto-translated into Chinese, with more languages planned. Prices show in 150+ currencies. All 14 UK regulated allergens and dietary preferences are fully filterable, so international students and diners with restrictions can eat with you instead of walking past.

A free public link you can share

Every venue gets a page at menuva.co.uk/menus. Share it on Instagram, WhatsApp, or Google Maps, print it on flyers, or use it as your table QR. Diners do not need the app to view it.

Instant menu changes

Update a price, mark an item out of stock, or add a seasonal special. Diners see the change within seconds. No reprinting, no waiting.

Analytics (coming soon)

In-portal analytics are launching soon. In the meantime, email hello@menuva.co.uk any time for a report covering views, engagement, filter usage, and top items.

3. Menu creation support

Our team will assist new venues in onboarding their menu to remove the initial admin burden. Afterwards, onboarding is as easy as editing your menu in the admin portal whenever things change.

We accept a wide range of file formats with no strict restriction. Images, PDFs, Excel spreadsheets, Word documents, PowerPoint decks, photos of printed menus, or links to your existing menu are all fine. Send whatever you already have. This helps us understand real-world inputs and automate onboarding for future venues.

Required information

  • Item name
  • Item price

Optional information

Providing any of these makes your menu more complete on day one.

  • Item descriptions
  • Allergens and dietary tags
  • Food hygiene rating
  • Sections and subsections for organizing the menu

Images. Any aspect ratio is accepted. The menu renders best with a widescreen header image (advertising graphic or a photo of the venue) and square item images (one per dish). If your aspect ratios differ, our team will resize them for you.

Customizations. Sides, add-ons, size variations, or any modifiers. For each customization group, include the option names, option prices, minimum and maximum quantities allowed, and whether the group is required or optional.

Email everything to hello@menuva.co.uk. Menu setup is free and will remain free.

4. How to apply

Email hello@menuva.co.uk with the following.

  • Full address

  • Proof of authority (business registration, Companies House listing, or a link showing your role on the venue's channels)

We review applications weekly. Once approved, pick either option below.

5. Managing your menu

We handle it (recommended)

Send your menu materials to hello@menuva.co.uk. We build, translate, and publish. When something changes, email us or update the portal yourself. Fastest way to go live.

You manage it yourself

Visit admin.menuva.co.uk and sign in with Google or any email (passwordless). The portal walks you through venue, hours, location, and items. Every change appears to diners instantly.

2. 为什么加入 Menuva

Menuva 是华威大学的一个 学生项目，目前没有任何营收，也不开展商业活动。平台对餐厅完全免费，且将长期保持免费。

让更多顾客发现您

目前约有 2,000 名华威顾客在使用 Menuva，每周都有新用户加入。83% 的顾客会在到店前查阅菜单，加入平台意味着您的菜单会在他们决定去哪里用餐的关键时刻直接出现在他们面前。

带动到店率与客单价

在线可见的菜单可使到店率提升约 10%。附有图片、描述和过敏原信息的菜单能稳定提高平均客单价，因为顾客到店前就已做好选择，也更有信心加点配菜、饮料或甜点。

触达国际顾客与特殊饮食顾客

菜单自动翻译为中文，后续将支持更多语言；价格可转换为 150+ 种货币；14 种英国法定过敏原及饮食偏好均可筛选。这意味着国际学生和有饮食限制的顾客会选择走进您的店，而不是路过。

一条可任意分享的免费链接

每家餐厅都会获得一个在 menuva.co.uk/menus 的公开页面。可分享到小红书、微信、Instagram、Google Maps，印在传单上，或用作桌面二维码。顾客无需下载 App 即可查看。

即时更新菜单

修改价格、标记售罄或添加季节性菜品后，顾客数秒内即可看到更新。无需重印，无需等待。

数据分析（即将上线）

后台数据分析功能即将推出。在此之前，欢迎随时发送邮件至 hello@menuva.co.uk 索取报告，内容涵盖浏览量、互动数据、筛选使用情况及热门菜品。

3. 菜单创建支持

我们的团队会协助新入驻餐厅完成菜单上线，帮您省去最初的资料整理负担。完成后，日常维护也非常简单，只需在菜品有变动时到管理后台更新即可。

我们接受多种文件格式，没有严格限制：图片、PDF、Excel 表格、Word 文档、PowerPoint、印刷菜单的照片，或现有菜单的链接均可。您现有什么材料就发给我们什么。这也帮助我们了解真实的输入情况，从而在未来为新餐厅提供更自动化的入驻流程。

必需信息

  • 菜品名称
  • 菜品价格

可选信息

以下内容任意提供都会让您的菜单在上线首日更完整、更吸引人。

  • 菜品描述
  • 过敏原和饮食标签
  • 食品卫生评级
  • 菜单分区和子分区，便于整理

图片。 任何比例均可接受。菜单在横向主图（宣传图或餐厅照片）和方形菜品图片（每道菜一张）下效果最佳。若您的图片比例不符，我们的团队会为您调整。

定制选项。 包括配菜、加料、规格或任意附加选项。请为每个定制组提供选项名称、选项价格、最小与最大可选数量，以及该组是必选还是可选。

请将以上材料发送至 hello@menuva.co.uk。菜单搭建服务免费，且将长期保持免费。

5. 菜单维护方式

由我们代为搭建（推荐）

将菜单资料发送至 hello@menuva.co.uk。我们会完成搭建、翻译并上线。后续有变动时，您可以邮件告知我们，或自行在管理后台更新。这是上线最快的方式。

自行维护

访问 admin.menuva.co.uk，使用 Google 或任意邮箱登录（邮箱登录无需密码）。后台会引导您添加餐厅、营业时间、位置和菜单项目。任何改动都会即时同步到顾客端。
