2. Why join Menuva

Menuva is a student-led project at the University of Warwick with no revenue and no commercial activity. It is free for restaurants today and will stay free.

Active, engaged diners

2,000+ Warwick diners already use Menuva, with new users every week. Your menu lands in front of them before they decide where to eat.

Real foot traffic

Diners browse from their accommodation, on the walk over, or at the bus stop. Complete menus drive pre-arrival decisions, so diners turn up ready to order.

Instant updates

Change a price, mark an item out of stock, or add a seasonal special. Diners see it within seconds.

A shareable public link

Every venue gets a page at menuva.co.uk/menus. Share it on Instagram, WhatsApp, or Google Maps. Print it on flyers. No app required to view.

International reach

Auto-translated into Chinese, more languages planned. Prices in 150+ currencies. All 14 UK regulated allergens and 7 dietary preferences fully filterable.

Analytics (coming soon)

In-portal analytics are launching soon. In the meantime, email hello@menuva.co.uk any time for a report covering views, engagement, filter usage, and top items.

3. Menu creation support

Free, hands-on setup for every new venue. Our team builds your initial menu from whatever you send. Afterwards you only need to edit in the admin portal when things change.

What to send

PriorityInformation
RequiredItem name and price
RecommendedHero and item images
RecommendedDescriptions
RecommendedAllergens and dietary tags
RecommendedCustomisations (name, price, min/max, required)
RecommendedFood hygiene rating
RecommendedSections and subsections

More detail means a more complete menu on day one. Email everything to hello@menuva.co.uk.

Cost

Free. Setup, hosting, translations, analytics, and every platform feature are free today and will stay free for restaurants.

4. How to apply

If you have been pre-selected

Reply to the email from our team. We take it from there.

If you are nominating your venue

Email hello@menuva.co.uk with the following.

  • Venue name
  • Full address
  • Opening hours
  • Relevant links (website, Instagram, existing menu, Google Maps)
  • Proof of authority (business registration, Companies House listing, or a link showing your role on the venue's channels)

We review applications weekly. Once approved, pick either option below.

5. Managing your menu

We handle it (recommended)

Send your menu materials to hello@menuva.co.uk. We build, translate, and publish. Email us when things change, or update the portal yourself. Fastest way to go live.

You manage it yourself

Visit admin.menuva.co.uk and sign in with Google or any email (passwordless). The portal walks you through venue, hours, location, and items. Every change appears to diners instantly.

6. What to expect

  1. Menuva is in its early stages. The admin portal and onboarding flow are actively being refined.
  2. Run into an issue or spot something we can improve? Email hello@menuva.co.uk. Your feedback directly shapes the platform.

2. 为什么加入 Menuva

Menuva 是华威大学的一个 学生项目，目前没有任何营收，也不开展商业活动。平台对餐厅完全免费，且将长期保持免费。

活跃的真实用户

已有 2,000+ 华威用户使用 Menuva，每周都有新用户加入。您的菜单将在他们决定去哪里用餐之前直接出现在他们面前。

真实的到店流量

顾客在宿舍、步行途中或公交站就会打开 Menuva。完整的菜单能影响他们的到店决定，让顾客到店即可下单。

即时更新

修改价格、标记售罄或添加季节性菜品后，顾客数秒内即可看到更新。

一条可分享的公开链接

每家餐厅都会获得一个在 menuva.co.uk/menus 的公开页面。可分享到小红书、微信、Instagram、Google Maps，或印在传单上。顾客无需下载 App 即可查看。

触达国际顾客

自动翻译为中文，后续将支持更多语言。价格可转换为 150+ 种货币。14 种英国法定过敏原和 7 种饮食偏好均可筛选。

数据分析（即将上线）

后台数据分析功能即将推出。在此之前，欢迎随时发送邮件至 hello@menuva.co.uk 索取报告，内容涵盖浏览量、互动数据、筛选使用情况及热门菜品。

3. 菜单创建支持

每家新入驻餐厅均可享受免费的菜单搭建服务。 我们的团队会根据您提供的资料搭建初版菜单。后续只需在菜品有变动时到管理后台做简单更新。

请发送给我们的内容

优先级信息
必需菜品名称和价格
建议主图和菜品图片
建议菜品描述
建议过敏原和饮食标签
建议定制选项（名称、价格、最小/最大数量、是否必选）
建议食品卫生评级
建议菜单分区和子分区

信息越详细，菜单从上线第一天起就越完整。请将以上材料发送至 hello@menuva.co.uk。

费用

免费。菜单搭建、托管、翻译、数据分析及所有平台功能目前均为免费，并将长期对餐厅免费开放。

4. 如何申请

若您已被预先邀请

请回复我们团队发给您的邮件，其余交给我们处理即可。

若您希望自荐加入

请发送邮件至 hello@menuva.co.uk，并在邮件中包含以下信息。

  • 餐厅名称
  • 完整地址
  • 营业时间
  • 相关链接（官网、Instagram、现有菜单、Google Maps 等）
  • 身份证明（营业执照、公司注册信息，或能证明您是官方代表的链接）

我们每周审核一次申请。通过后，您可在以下两种方式中任选其一。

5. 菜单维护方式

由我们代为搭建（推荐）

将菜单资料发送至 hello@menuva.co.uk。我们会完成搭建、翻译并上线。后续有变动时，您可以邮件告知我们，或自行在管理后台更新。这是上线最快的方式。

自行维护

访问 admin.menuva.co.uk，使用 Google 或任意邮箱登录（邮箱登录无需密码）。后台会引导您添加餐厅、营业时间、位置和菜单项目。任何改动都会即时同步到顾客端。

6. 注意事项

  1. Menuva 仍处于早期阶段。管理后台和入驻流程正在持续优化中。
  2. 如您遇到任何问题或发现可改进之处，请发送邮件至 hello@menuva.co.uk。您的反馈将直接影响平台的发展。
