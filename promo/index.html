4.3 The discount does not apply to:

  • service charges, tips, or gratuities
  • items not selected within Menuva

4.4 The discount cannot be exchanged for cash, credit, or any alternative.

5. How to redeem

To redeem the discount, you must, at the point of payment at a participating venue, present Menuva on your device in one of the following ways:

  • by displaying the Menuva order summary screen for the items being purchased, or
  • by displaying the Menuva screen showing the relevant venue and the item(s) for which the discount is being applied.

Venues are under no obligation to apply the discount in such circumstances.
