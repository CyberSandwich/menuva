Menuva is currently accepting selected requests to join the platform. Self-nomination will be available soon.

## 1. How to apply

1. Visit [admin.menuva.co.uk](https://admin.menuva.co.uk) and sign in using Google or any email address. Email sign-in is passwordless.
2. Email [hello@menuva.co.uk](mailto:hello@menuva.co.uk) with the email address used to sign in and proof that you are the owner or authorised representative of a legitimate food venue (for example, a business registration certificate, a Companies House listing, or a link to your venue's official website).
3. Once your access is approved, the admin portal will guide you through adding your venue, including its name, opening hours, location, and menu items.

## 2. Menu creation support

Our team can support the creation of an initial draft of your menu. If you would like us to process your menu on your behalf, email [hello@menuva.co.uk](mailto:hello@menuva.co.uk) with the following.

**Required (minimum):**

- Item name and price for each menu item.

**Optional (recommended where available):**

- Hero images and item images.
- Item descriptions.
- Allergen and dietary information.
- Customizations, including option names, prices, minimum and maximum quantities, and whether each selection is mandatory or optional.
- Food hygiene rating.
- Sections and subsections for menu organisation.

The more detail you provide, the more complete your menu will be from day one.

## 3. What to expect

1. Menuva is in its early stages. The admin portal and onboarding experience are actively being refined.
2. If you encounter any issues, find that the experience can be improved, or discover a bug at any point, please let [hello@menuva.co.uk](mailto:hello@menuva.co.uk) know as soon as possible. Your feedback directly shapes the platform.
