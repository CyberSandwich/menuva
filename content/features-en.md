This is a brief list of features. It will be updated in the future.

- Free to use
- No account needed
- Filter allergens
- Preview in advance
- Show your digital basket to order
- Sort by price and nutrition
- Map view with open/closed indicators
- Convert prices
- Search
- Edit items
- English (UK) and Chinese
- Dark/light mode
