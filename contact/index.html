3. Common reasons to contact us

  • A venue menu looks out of date, missing, or incorrect.
  • Allergen, ingredient, nutrition, translation, or price display looks wrong.
  • App, admin portal, or website bug, crash, slow performance, or UI issue.
  • Accessibility feedback (screen reader, contrast, motion, text size).
  • Venue partnership and onboarding.
  • Supplier or partner-service enquiry.
