Menuva is not publicly advertising any specific role openings. That said, we are always looking for exceptional people who want to help build the future default platform for menus, internationally.

1. About menuva

1.1 Menuva launched on 12 January 2026 at the University of Warwick and has grown to over 500 users at the time of writing. Our ambition is to rapidly expand to more universities and beyond.

1.2 Menuva is a student-led project and is not a registered business. Joining the team does not constitute formal employment and there is no salary. What you will get is the opportunity to work on a real product with real users, build skills that matter, and create genuine impact.

2. What we are looking for

We are looking for highly proactive individuals with a track record of taking initiative. You do not need to be an expert in anything. What matters most is your drive to learn, upskill, and deliver results.

Growth

Venue growth and user growth. You would work closely with restaurants, cafes, societies, student unions, university departments, and other organisations to bring menuva to more people and more places.

Technical excellence

Building and improving the product across our stack. Experience in any of the following is a plus, but not required:

  • React Native
  • Kotlin
  • Swift
  • HTML, CSS, JavaScript
  • Firebase
  • React
  • Python

3. University leads

We are expanding to other universities and are looking for university leads to take charge of menuva's presence at their institution. As a university lead, you would:

  • Recruit and manage a local team of growth and operations members.
  • Drive venue partnerships and user adoption on campus.
  • Work closely with societies, the student union, university departments, and local businesses.
  • Represent menuva at your university and help shape our expansion strategy.

4. How to apply

There is no formal interview process. Those who would like to join the team will be asked to meet Duke (founder) in person.

To express your interest, send an email to hello@menuva.co.uk with the following:

  • Your name.
  • Your role or degree, including year of study.
  • Three sentences about a past initiative you led or drove forward.
  • Anything else you think is relevant.

You will hear back as soon as possible. No ghosting, guaranteed.
